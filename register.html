How the register is meant to work

  • Every figure shown in the planner points to one row here. A figure with no verified date does not display; the topic shows the rule in words and the flag instead.
  • A row past its recheck trigger turns stale and raises the same flag on every topic that uses it.
  • Referral names, phone numbers and eligibility notes are rows too. The planner currently shows five helpline numbers (OPAN, My Aged Care, 1800 ELDERHelp, National Debt Helpline, Carer Gateway) that no one has checked yet, so each carries a verify flag.
  • Figures for ages 12 to 81 are copied from coeconomics.com, the original planner. They display with a note that they have not been checked against ABS, AIHW, ATO, ASFA or Services Australia, and rows C24 to C34 track that.
  • The map and its topics are general information only. The flag on a topic never means a figure is wrong, only that no person has confirmed it yet.
